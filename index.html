01 — About

Engineering the systems behind the model.

I'm a software engineer who lives at the intersection of ML infrastructure, distributed systems, and generative AI. At EOG Resources, I build the self-service platforms, GPU training pipelines, and inference services that let researchers and data scientists ship models without fighting infrastructure — and the LLM & vision pipelines that turn unstructured field data into real-time decisions.

My academic background spans an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Houston and an M.S. in Software Systems from the Coimbatore Institute of Technology, with published research on AR-based safety training and cloud data integrity. I'm a Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD), and I write about data engineering and distributed systems on Medium.

Most days I'm somewhere between a Kubernetes manifest and a half-finished Medium draft — I like the kind of problems that only show up once a system is actually running at scale.
